Help & Creative Harness Architecture

Comprehensive reference guide on how the AI Harness coordinates specialized agents and breaks creative roadblocks.

The Creative Harness Philosophy

Generic AI chatbots fail creators because they lack domain context, produce ungrounded generic prose, and hallucinate without self-auditing. Generative Clash solves this by deploying profession-specific harness layers:

  1. Domain Personalization: Selecting your category (Writers, Musicians, Creators, Designers) arms specialized agent personas.
  2. Intent & Problem Isolation: The dispatcher decomposes your prompt to identify whether you need structural expansion, tone refinement, or creative unblocking.
  3. Continuous Verification: Outputs are monitored against craft constraints before display.
